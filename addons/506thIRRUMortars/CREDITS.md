Used as a dependency (`69C6E7E5C3006233` in `addon.gproj`). This mod does not redistribute
any of its content; it overrides the M224A1 deployed mortar prefab by GUID to add the
`IRRU_MortarArtilleryComputerComponent` and its user action, and carries a transcription of
the mod's published firing card (FM 10-M224.1) in `MortarBallisticTables.c`.

**Permission:** on 2026-08-22 Fedora12 (506IRRU) asked Krios in Discord for formal permission
to (1) add the mod as a dependency of 506IRRU Mortar Mod and (2) add a mortar computer to the
M224A1. Krios replied "sure". A screenshot of the exchange is kept by the 506IRRU mod team.
